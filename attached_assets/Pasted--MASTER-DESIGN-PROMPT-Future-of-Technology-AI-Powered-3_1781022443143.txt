# 🚀 MASTER DESIGN PROMPT

## Future of Technology – AI-Powered 3D Interactive Experience

Design and develop a world-class futuristic 3D web experience called **"Future of Technology"** for a Techfest IIT Bombay Campus Ambassador web development challenge.

The website should feel like a premium technology exhibition from the future, combining cutting-edge design, immersive 3D experiences, modern web technologies, and compelling storytelling.

The final result should feel like a blend of:

* Apple Product Launch Experience
* Iron Man's Jarvis Interface
* Tesla Design Philosophy
* Cyberpunk 2077 Visuals
* MIT Media Lab Innovation
* Techfest IIT Bombay Spirit

The website must instantly impress judges with exceptional creativity, visual quality, interaction design, and technical execution.

---

# 🎯 Project Objective

Create an immersive interactive journey through humanity's most transformative technologies.

The experience should educate, inspire, and amaze visitors while showcasing advanced frontend development skills.

Visitors should feel like they are exploring a futuristic digital technology ecosystem rather than scrolling through a traditional website.

---

# 🌌 Core Concept

At the center of the experience exists an intelligent entity called:

## AI Core

The AI Core represents the convergence of all future technologies.

Every technology domain in the website is connected to this central intelligence.

Visualize it as:

```text
                 Space Technology

                        ●

                        │

                        │

Cybersecurity ● ─── AI CORE ─── Robotics

                        │

                        │

                        ●

                 Smart Cities

                        │

                        ●

             Autonomous Mobility
```

The AI Core should visually feel alive.

It should:

* Rotate continuously
* Emit energy pulses
* Generate particles
* React to user interactions
* Connect all technology domains
